Camera & Microphone Access

SignBridge needs your camera to recognize sign language gestures, and optionally your microphone to capture the speaker's voice. Your video is processed locally and never stored.

Live

Start a real-time conversation using your camera. SignBridge recognizes your signs and translates them into spoken English, enabling seamless communication between deaf and hearing individuals.

Demo

Walk through a pre-written conversation between a deaf signer and a hearing person. Follow along step-by-step to see how SignBridge translates signs into spoken English in real time.

Upload

Upload a pre-recorded signing video — a story, a monologue, a recorded conversation turn — and get back a multi-sentence narrative transcript. Each sign is recognized individually, then an LLM stitches them into well-formed English sentences.

Speak responses aloud

                        
Synth voice reads the signer's caption to the hearing person. Off = staff reads from the screen.

                    

                    
                


                

                    

                        
Chime when translation quality drops

                        
Plays a short tone when CTQI falls below the alert threshold (Advanced section).

                    

                    
                


                

                    

                        
Speaker wake word

                        
Say this word to start a spoken reply (e.g. "Signer, I'd like a coffee"). The wake word is stripped from the transcript. Single word only.

                    

                    
                


                

                    

                        
Speaker language

                        
Translate the signer's English caption into this language before display + TTS on Send. The original English stays visible beneath the translation. English = no translation (no extra LLM call).

                    

                    
                

            


            
            

                
Advanced


                

                    

                        
CTQI alert threshold

                        
Captions with CTQI below this value get a red pulsing border + optional chime. 0–100, default 80.

                    

                    
                


                

                    

                        
CTQI hard floor

                        
Captions with CTQI below this value are replaced with [unclear] instead of showing the constructed sentence (the model is essentially guessing). CTQI badge + Regenerate / Edit chips still appear so the signer can rescue the message. 0–100, default 40.
