Notes
-----
- "Active glosses" is what SignBridge can actually predict per-domain at
  inference time. Masked class IDs are produced by the trainer for glosses
  that did not meet quality thresholds (e.g. too few training samples after
  the train-split anti-leakage filter) and are zeroed out of the softmax
  inside load_model_from_checkpoint().
- At inference, every domain model runs alongside the Common Words
  companion model (32 active glosses), so the effective vocabulary the user
  sees per domain is roughly: <domain active> + 32 (minus any overlap).
- The Fallback model is loaded when no domain-specific model is requested.
- Sums in the TOTALS row are arithmetic only; they double-count any glosses
  that appear in multiple domain models (e.g. common-words overlap).
